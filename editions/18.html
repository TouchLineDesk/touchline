The January edition · Season 2 · No. 7

The pack arrives

Michigan started January two points clear of North Carolina and finish it two points clear of three different clubs, with a game in hand they are playing as this edition goes to print. Texas won all five of their games and scored thirty-six goals doing it, fifteen of them in one afternoon against Duke; they climb from seventh to second. Miami beat Michigan 5–0 and did not concede a goal all month. Alabama won five from five as well. Five clubs now sit within four points of the leaders. And on Signing Day, Daniel Garrett chose Michigan.

January

December 31 (evening) to January 31 · league games only

The month in one table: what each of the Sixteen did between the two reads. Texas and Alabama won everything they played; Miami won everything and conceded nothing. North Carolina, second on New Year’s Eve, are sixth.

15/15 Texas

Seventh to second

Five games, five wins: 5–1 against UCLA on New Year’s Day, 10–0 at Clemson, 15–1 against Duke, 4–1 against LSU and a 2–1 at Washington won in the 92nd minute by Josh Sargent. Texas now have the most league goals in the division (119), one more than Michigan.

31–0 Miami

The perfect defence

Four games, four wins, nothing conceded: 5–0 against Michigan, 4–0 at USC, 5–0 at Akron and 17–0 against Oregon. Miami have the best goal difference in the league (+89) and the fewest goals against (26).

15/15 Alabama

The quiet perfect month

Five wins from five, twenty goals of difference, and up four places to seventh. Sixteen-year-olds José Manuel Sánchez and David Martin had seventeen goal involvements between them in all competitions; Will Ockford, also sixteen, scored seven in all competitions.

9/12 Michigan

Still top, barely

Beat Ohio State 4–3, lost 5–0 at Miami, beat UCLA and Clemson. Nine points from four games, and the lead is the same two points it was on New Year’s Eve. What changed is how many clubs are behind it: three, on 58.

2nd→6th North Carolina

The slide

Seven points from five games, two defeats in the last two weekends (3–1 at Oklahoma, 4–2 at home to Georgia), and down four places. Robert Korshunov scored ten in January in all competitions; it was not enough.

4 pts Texas Tech · Florida State · USC

The three who stalled

Four points each from January. Texas Tech lost 5–0 at Georgia and 7–2 at Alabama and drop from sixth to tenth; USC from tenth to twelfth; Florida State from ninth to eleventh.

The Garrett file, No. 6 · Signing Day

The last instalment · file closed

Michigan

Daniel Garrett has declared for Michigan.

That is the whole of the news, and after six months of this desk printing what it could stand up and what it could not, it is a relief to print something that needs no second column. The inverted wing back from Livonia, who sat in the main stand in Ann Arbor on November 12, spent the winter playing indoor football half an hour from the stadium, and was asked about by people around Texas and UCLA, will play his college football at the club that is top of the table.

He announced it the way he has done everything we have seen him do: briefly. Jess Ocampo, who called his Under-15 games on Touchline Live in the autumn, asked him why.

“I said Michigan was the closest to home and that it didn’t mean anything yet. It means something now. They told me I’d play wing back, either side. That’s what I wanted to hear.” — Daniel Garrett

His father was asked whether the family had ever seriously looked elsewhere.

“He made the decision. I drove.” — his father

Whether Texas or UCLA ever made an offer, we still do not know, and we are not going to find out now. Michigan have not said a word on the record in six months, and they did not start today.

The file is closed. From here Daniel Garrett is a Michigan player, and he will be covered like every other Michigan player: by what the save says he does. When he appears in the save he gets a Touchline card, a page in the Almanac and a line in The Next Ones. Until then, this is the last time we will write about him without a number next to his name.

The tables, results, every appearance, goal, assist, rating and ability figure on this page were read from the save on January 31, 2023, at 19:30, before Michigan v Duke. Player statistics are all competitions unless a line says league; monthly player figures include January’s midweek games outside the league. Head coaches are referred to by role only. Real players appear only through their on-pitch record in the save. Club crests are Touchline’s own designs in each school’s colours, not official marks.

Touchline Grades, the power rankings, the Team of the Month, the Touchline Exchange, Record Watch, the Rivalry Index, the 2023 board and the mock draft are this desk’s own models, not figures the game produces. Season 1 records and history come from Touchline’s own Season 1 files. The mock uses today’s USLP table as a stand-in for a draft lottery that has been sealed but not run.

Daniel Garrett is a fiction inside a fiction. He does not yet exist in the save, and neither do his father, Livonia Metro, Touchline Live or Jess Ocampo. The Garrett file was a storyline this desk built in the open; it closes in this edition with his decision for Michigan. When he is added to the save he will be covered like any other player, by his numbers.

All quotes are invented for this fictional universe. Quotes come only from Touchline’s fictional panel and from Daniel Garrett and his father, who are fictional. No real person is quoted.
