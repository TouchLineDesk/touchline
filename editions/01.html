FIFA Youth League · Final

The world's final four was all American

128 of the planet's best youth sides entered. Four USL Academy clubs reached the semi-finals. On May 15, North Carolina and Michigan play for the title in Italy.

Touchline Academy Desk

Barcelona were beaten by UCLA twice in the group stage, and then again by Florida, 6–2. Georgia took Manchester United's under-23s apart 6–1 and eliminated Arsenal's. Michigan beat Paris Saint-Germain's under-19s twice and put 12 past Monterrey. UCLA, North Carolina, Michigan and Georgia each won all six group games.

By April the competition had become a domestic affair played abroad. Florida knocked Georgia out in extra time, 3–2. Michigan won the first semi-final 3–2 in Gainesville. In the second, league champions UCLA drew 1–1 with North Carolina and went out on penalties, ending their hopes of a double.

The final is a contrast in styles. North Carolina have conceded twice in 11 Youth League games. Michigan have scored 61. This season, Michigan have not beaten the Tar Heels: they lost 4–1 and drew 2–2 in preseason, then lost 2–0 in the league. Thirteen days after the final, the two meet again in the league.

Champions

No stars. No problem. UCLA are champions.

UCLA have the lowest-rated squad of the Sixteen and no player rated above 131. They won the title by 11 points, did the double over seven of their fifteen rivals, and topped the table of results between the Sixteen with 63 points from 28 games. Only North Carolina beat them twice.

10pts
Tennessee, one behind Duke with two to play
Relegation

Tennessee's survival runs through Alabama

Michigan State are already down after 38 defeats in 40. One more place drops to the College Championship League. Duke have 11 points and go to Oregon and Oklahoma. Tennessee have 10 and visit Alabama on Sunday, then finish at winless Michigan State. Alabama's board expects a top-five finish, and they are sixth. They can't afford to be generous.

104
Michigan's average ability, highest of the Sixteen
The paradox

Seventh at home. One win from the world.

On paper, Michigan have the most talented squad in the league. They have one win in their last five league games and are fighting to reach the top five. They have also scored 61 goals in 11 Youth League games and play for its title on May 15. Four days after Ohio State thrashed them 4–1, Michigan beat them 3–1 at home in the Rose Bowl quarter-final.

The race for the world

UCLA (99) and North Carolina (88) are through. Solid bars show current points, and the hatched area shows the most each club can reach with two wins. Ohio State (72) can reach 78 at most and are out.

Miami and USC are the league's two hottest teams: five straight wins for Miami and four for USC. They meet on Saturday. USC's run-in is the hardest of anyone's: Miami away, then Alabama away on the final day. Holders of the Youth League may also get an automatic place, which isn't confirmed yet. If they do and Michigan win on May 15 but finish outside the top five, it would make six American clubs next season.

Feuds, by the numbers

Rivalries written by results, not tradition
UNC 2 wins · UCLA 0 · 1 draw, UNC through on penalties

The champions' kryptonite

UCLA lost only five league games to the Sixteen, and two of them were against North Carolina (4–3, 3–1). Then the Tar Heels ended UCLA's Youth League run on penalties after a 1–1 draw. Whatever the champions have, UNC seem to have the answer.

Florida 3 · Alabama 0 · aggregate 9–2

Gainesville owns Alabama

Florida won 4–1 in the league, 4–1 in SEC play and 1–0 in February. Georgia also beat Alabama 6–1 on New Year's Day. Alabama had beaten Georgia 3–1 in August and 5–2 in SEC play. Against Florida they have nothing.

Florida 2 · Georgia 2 · 1 draw · goals 10–12

The most even fight in the league

They have met five times: two preseason wins for Georgia, a 2–2 draw, a 3–2 Florida win in the league, and a 3–2 extra-time win for Florida in the Youth League quarter-final that knocked Georgia off the world stage.

UCLA 2 · USC 0 · goals 8–1

Los Angeles belongs to UCLA

UCLA won 2–0 at home and 6–1 at USC. The Trojans are one of the form teams in the league, but not across town.

Texas 3 · Oklahoma 0

Red River, one-way traffic

Texas won 4–2 and 3–1 in the league and 3–0 in Big 12 play. Texas have lost four of their last five league games, but they found a way against Oklahoma.

Touchline

Touchline is fiction. It's an in-universe sports desk for a Football Manager 2022 save. Every result, table and statistic comes from the save as of the in-game date of May 4, 2022. Head coaches are referred to by role only. Real players appear only through their on-pitch record in the save.

All quotes are invented for this fictional universe. Quotes come only from fictional players (the game's generated youngsters), from coaches identified by role, and from Touchline's fictional panel. No real person is quoted. The panel's opinions of real players cover only their performances in the save.
