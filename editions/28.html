Season 3 · No. 1 · the opening fortnight

Twenty-one, and counting

The league opened on Saturday, August 19, and in twelve days it has produced 208 goals in 35 games, three 4–4 draws, an 8–0 with five goals from one player, a 9–0 and a 5–4. Florida State are the only side with four wins, and the fourth of them came with ten men. Michigan have 21 goals and the model’s favouritism. Georgia have three wins and a goal a game less than last season. Miami, the June model’s third pick, are fifteenth. A fifteen-year-old has seven assists. This is the first full edition of Season 3 and the first update on the sixteen storylines, which is at the bottom, where it will be every month.

Round by round

August 19 to August 30 · 35 games · 208 goals

Four rounds in twelve days. The Sixteen played the six clubs outside the Sixteen sixteen times and won all sixteen, by an aggregate of 100–5. Between themselves the Sixteen played seventeen games and drew three, all of them 4–4. The two games between the six were both 0–0. The goals are below, round by round.

9–0Clemson v Michigan, Aug 30

Folarin Balogun scored twice and Gilbert Fuentes three times. Daniel Garrett scored the eighth, his first of the season, at 56 minutes. Michigan have now won 6–1, 6–1 and 9–0.

4–5Texas v Florida State, Aug 26

Nicholas Gioacchini scored at 9, 11 and 45; Florida State led 4–0 at the break. Texas scored four in the second half, two from Ariel Mbumba, the last in the 89th minute, and still lost.

8–0Oklahoma v TCU, Aug 19

Jackson Conway: a penalty in the first minute, then 17, 19, 27 and 53. Five goals on opening day. He has played 88 minutes of the season and scored six.

3–2Florida State v Florida, Aug 30

The Sunshine Anchor. Florida led twice, through Trey Corke and Scott Lira, Landon Pledger was sent off at 63 with the score 2–2, and Florida State won it anyway: Wilfredo Rivera at 85, with ten men. The anchor stays in Tallahassee.

Sat Aug 19 · Sun Aug 20Opening weekend

Eleven games, 62 goals. Texas won 6–1 at Clemson, USC 6–0 at Rice with four from Ken Aboh, Washington 7–1 at Louisville, Oklahoma 8–0 against TCU. Michigan beat Florida 6–1 with an Isaiah Foster hat-trick, a centre-back’s. Florida State beat Alabama 6–1 with three from Gioacchini. Georgia won 3–2 at North Carolina, the Youth League final rematch: Andrew Carleton put North Carolina ahead at 14, then Sergiño Dest at 36, Jose Villaseñor at 71, and after Jalen Hawkins had made it 2–2 at 84, Harrison Robledo at 90. On the Sunday Miami lost 0–1 at home to LSU to a Juan Castilla goal at 86, and Notre Dame won 5–1 at Tulane.

Wed Aug 23Midweek

Eight games, 51 goals. Florida 8–0 Louisville: hat-tricks for Missael Rodriguez and Daniel Rios. Alabama 6–0 TCU, four from Brandon Rochester. North Carolina 7–1 Rice. Texas Tech 4–4 LSU: Texas Tech came from 2–3 down to lead 4–3 through two from Sergio Alpuche in three minutes, then conceded to Oli Griffin at 84. Washington won 6–3 at Notre Dame with a Dan Dickinson hat-trick at sixteen. Florida State beat Ohio State 2–1 with two from Iván Meza, the first inside a minute; Ricardo Pepi pulled one back at 79.

Sat Aug 26Round three

Eleven games, 71 goals. Ohio State 9–0 Rice, Pepi and Patrick Weah three each. Alabama 8–0 Tulane, Caden Clark three. LSU 7–0 Louisville, Jesus Ferreira three. Florida beat North Carolina 5–2, Rios twice. USC beat Miami 5–1: Santiago Muñoz scored at 1, 5 and 7 minutes, the third a penalty. Michigan 6–1 at UCLA. Notre Dame 4–4 Texas Tech, and Washington 4–4 Oklahoma, Jackson Conway equalising at 90. Texas 4–5 Florida State. Georgia 2–0 Akron, both from Richard Ledezma inside eleven minutes.

Tue Aug 29 · Wed Aug 30Midweek

Texas beat Alabama 3–0 on the Tuesday: Federico Oliva at 4, then Jorgé Hernandez and Jonathan Gómez. On the Wednesday Michigan won 9–0 at Clemson, Miami beat North Carolina 5–2 with two from Fernando Sánchez and one from Lance Thompson, Florida State took the Sunshine Anchor 3–2, and Rice and Akron drew 0–0, the second goalless draw between two of the six clubs outside the Sixteen.

16 hat-tricks or better in 35 games: Conway 5, Aboh 4, Rochester 4, and thirteen threes, two of them Gioacchini’s. Two red cards: an unnamed Akron player at 61 against Ohio State, and Landon Pledger of Florida State at 63 against Florida. Two own goals, one of them by Gabriel Słonina, Alabama’s goalkeeper, for Florida State’s sixth.

Three games is not much evidence, and the model knows it; Michigan’s number has moved as far as it has because 21–2 is not a normal three games.
