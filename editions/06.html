Lottery night

The envelope opens

Twenty-four days ago Touchline published a sixty-four character fingerprint and refused to say what it meant. Tonight the seed behind it is on the table, the seventeen clubs of the USL Premier have an order, and sixty-seven young men know the exact minute their name gets called.

01
Verify the envelope

Run SHA-256 over the seed below. It has to produce the fingerprint published on May 6, before a single graduate had been counted and before the Premier table was final. If it does, the seed was fixed then.

02
Take the field

Sixty-seven graduates means seventeen drafting clubs — the top seventeen of the final USL Premier table. That field was locked on May 14 when Philadelphia finished eighteenth on goal difference.

03
Key every club

For each club, run SHA-256 over the seed, a colon, and the club’s name exactly as the Premier table prints it. That produces seventeen sixty-four character keys.

04
Sort, then snake

Sort the keys low to high. That is round one. Round two runs backwards, round three forwards, round four backwards again and stops when the graduates run out — sixteen picks, so the club that won the lottery is the only one that does not pick in it.

Sixty-seven graduates, sixty-seven picks. Nobody in this class goes undrafted — the last name read out in Dallas still gets a professional contract, and it belongs to LA Galaxy, whose city also owns the first pick of the night.

Who won the night

Table finish against lottery slot
The winners

The best team in the league gets the best pick

LAFC

LAFC finished first in the Premier by two goals of difference and then drew the first key out of the hat. They hold the first pick of the draft and, because of the turn, the thirty-fourth and thirty-fifth as well — back-to-back selections at the top of round three. The one tax the system charges them is real: the lottery winner is the club that loses the partial fourth round, so LAFC leave Dallas with three names while everybody else leaves with four.

The riser

Sixteenth of seventeen, fourth in the order

D.C. United

D.C. United scraped into the field at all — fifty-six points, plus nine, a goal difference ahead of Philadelphia and behind Inter Miami on nothing much. Twelve places is the biggest jump of the night. Cincinnati, seventeenth into the field on the same margin, came out fifth. Between them they turned two of the last three tickets into two of the first five picks.

The faller

Seventh in the table, seventeenth in the order

San Jose

San Jose had the seventh-best season in the Premier and drew the highest key of the seventeen. There is a consolation and it is a genuine one: the club at the end of the order picks back-to-back at each turn. Seventeen and eighteen, then fifty-one and fifty-two. Two pairs of consecutive picks is a tradeable, usable asset. It is not the first pick.

The hosts

Dallas host the draft and pick twelfth

FC Dallas

The building is theirs and the twelfth pick is theirs, which means eleven names get read out in front of a home crowd before FC Dallas get to say one. They moved up a single place from thirteenth. New York did not move at all — fourteenth in the table, fourteenth in the order, the only club in the field the lottery declined to have an opinion about.

The panel

Reaction, live from the desk
DR
Dale Rourke
Rourke Unfiltered
Everybody wants me to be angry that the best team in the league just won the lottery. I’m not angry, I’m delighted. This is the only sport in America honest enough to say out loud that the rich get richer. And before somebody writes in — yes, I spent three weeks saying a lottery was a coward’s way of setting an order. It is. It’s also produced the single most entertaining result it could have produced. That’s called range.
On the rich getting richer
NO
Nadia Okafor
The Whiteboard
Look at what is actually on the board rather than who picks first. Twenty-five of the sixty-seven graduates are centre-backs or full-backs. Nine are goalkeepers. This is a defensive class, and the clubs that came out of the lottery well are mostly clubs who needed attackers. Somebody in the top five is going to take the fifth-best player on the board because he plays the position they can’t fill, and in eighteen months nobody will remember that it looked like a reach.
On a defensive class
TB
Theo Brandt
The Ledger
The first pick of the draft belongs to Los Angeles and so does the sixty-seventh. LAFC hold the first pick and two of the top thirty-five with one selection fewer than anybody else; weight the picks by where they fall and that is still the most valuable haul in the field by roughly a third. Then it flattens out violently. The last five clubs in the order hold four picks each worth within two and a half per cent of one another. The lottery decided one thing enormously and about a dozen things hardly at all.
On the shape of the order
WK
Walt Kearney
Kearney's Corner
I have covered this beat for forty years and I have never enjoyed writing the sentence I am about to write. In three weeks the Sixteen lose sixty-seven players in a single evening, including the best footballer any of us have watched in this league. Florida State lose seven and thirty per cent of their minutes. The champions lose six. We spend all season arguing about who is over-achieving and then the calendar walks in and empties the room.

I have had the board graded since the fourteenth of May and the top of it has not moved an inch through two regradings. Two A-pluses. Three As. Five A-minuses. Do the arithmetic and you will understand why the seventh pick matters more than anyone is saying. Circle the name.

Touchline is fiction. It’s an in-universe sports desk for a Football Manager 2022 save. Every result, table and statistic on this page comes from the save. Head coaches are referred to by role only. Real players appear only through their on-pitch record in the save. Club crests are Touchline’s own designs in each school’s colours, not official marks. The lottery is verifiable. The fingerprint above was published on May 6, 2022, in-game, before the draft field or the size of the class was known. The seed was written down at the same moment and not touched since. Run SHA-256 over the seed, check it against the fingerprint, then key each club and sort. The order on this page is the only order that seed can produce.

All quotes are invented for this fictional universe. Quotes come only from fictional players, from coaches identified by role, and from Touchline’s fictional panel. No real person is quoted.
