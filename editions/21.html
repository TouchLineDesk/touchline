The April edition · Season 2 · No. 10

Six for five

Michigan went into April level on points at the top and came out of it third, with seven points from five games: beaten 4–0 at Alabama, held 4–4 by Notre Dame, beaten 3–0 at Florida State on Saturday. Georgia lost at Alabama on the same afternoon and still lead, on 90. Texas lost twice and are second. Behind them the table has closed up: six points cover the top six, and every one of them has two league games left, on May 6 and May 27. The top five qualify for next season’s Youth League, so one of these six will miss it. Touchline ran the real fixtures 20,000 times to find which games decide that. The answer is three of them, and two belong to Miami.

Also in this edition: the 2023 draft has a home, Seattle, on June 20, and a new mark to go with it. A year on from Dallas we look back at the Class of 2022, redraft it, and name the steals and the reaches. And with every school’s signings now done, we look at what next season’s rosters will be once 84 graduates leave.

The run-in

Two games left · the real fixture list, simulated 20,000 times

Georgia are 93.2% to win the league, up from 59.3% a month ago. They play Florida at home on May 6 and finish at TCU; a win against Florida settles it unless Texas win both of theirs. Texas, two points behind, are the only realistic challengers, at 6.2%. Michigan, level with Georgia a month ago, are down to 0.6%.

The fight that is still open is for the five Youth League places. Six clubs are separated by six points. Georgia are in, in every simulation Touchline ran. Texas (95.5%) and Michigan (90.7%) are close to it. Oklahoma (79.6%), Alabama (78.4%) and Miami (55.7%) are the three clubs most likely to miss out. North Carolina and Texas Tech, on 80, can reach 86 but would need goal difference and four results to go their way; their chances round to zero.

Goal difference matters more than usual. Oklahoma (+102) and Alabama (+100) are level on 86 and play each other on May 6. Miami are two points behind both but have the best goal difference in the league, +129, so a tie on points goes to Miami.

Draft 23

Seattle · Tuesday, June 20 · 84 graduates · 21 drafting clubs

The 2023 USL Premier Draft will be held in Seattle on Tuesday, June 20. Dallas had the first one. Seattle gets the second: the Sounders are fourth in the USLP and a lock to draft, and the University of Washington, one of the Sixteen, is in the same city and sends six graduates into this class.

The mark follows last year’s: the same gold-edged shield, the ball on top of a tower and the city skyline behind. This year the tower is a needle with a saucer deck, with a mountain and the water behind it. Dallas’s red, navy and gold stay as the draft’s colours.

The rules are the same as in 2022. Every player at the Sixteen who is 23 on draft day (born on or before June 20, 2000) is drafted. The class is 84, unchanged since March: none of April’s 33 signings is old enough. That means 84 ÷ 4 = 21 drafting clubs and four full rounds, so this year every club gets exactly four picks. Last year the lottery winner got three.

April

April 1 to April 29 · league games only

Texas Tech and Florida State won all five, the only perfect records of the month. Alabama took 13 and beat both Michigan and Georgia. Miami took 12. Michigan took seven and went from first to third.
