FIFA Youth League final · Eve of the match

Italy first. Then everything else.

North Carolina and Michigan play for the world's youth crown on Sunday. Then each plays a bowl final on Wednesday. Then they meet again on May 28. No two Academy teams have ever had a fortnight like this.

By Jess Ocampo · Touchline

Both clubs spent the week warming up by demolition. Michigan went to the Rose Bowl and put seven past USC. Folarin Balogun scored a hat-trick in 31 minutes, and Chris Durkin scored in the 3rd minute and again in the 78th. North Carolina took their Fiesta Bowl semi-final 4–1 against Oklahoma, who had beaten them in the league only last week, with centre-back Ian Murphy scoring twice.

It sets up the final everyone has wanted since the draw. Michigan have 61 goals in 11 Youth League games. North Carolina have conceded two. Balogun arrives with 49 goals in all competitions, the most at any of the Sixteen. North Carolina's coach still hasn't lost to Michigan in three meetings, and he still says that means nothing.

Win or lose on Sunday, both fly home to a bowl final 72 hours later: North Carolina against Miami in the Fiesta, Michigan against champions UCLA in the Rose. The league meeting on May 28 decides nothing in the table. After this fortnight, it won't feel that way.

Alabama: half of "win twice"

Seven on Sunday, revenge on Wednesday, one door left
USL Academy · Sunday May 8
Alabama6th · 80 pts
7–0
Tennessee21st · relegated
Barco pen 8 · Fröling 10, 34 · Munjoma 61 · Montes 72 · Greene 76 · Vega 90+1
Swan sent off 71

The Alabama head coach said on Saturday night that his team had to win twice and then find out. On Sunday they won once, 7–0, and six different players scored. Ezequiel Barco put away a penalty in the 8th minute, Nils Fröling added two before half-time, and the defeat sent Tennessee down with Michigan State.

Then came Wednesday, and the thing Alabama hadn't managed all season: beating Florida. The Gators had won all three meetings, 9–2 on aggregate. In the Cotton Bowl semi-final Alabama won 3–1. Gennaro Nigro equalised, Brandon Rochester scored just before half-time and Devin Vega added the third.

The reward is a Cotton Bowl final on Wednesday against Georgia, who beat them 6–1 on New Year's Day. Twenty-four hours later the Alabama head coach, who also runs the national team, is due in Curaçao.
