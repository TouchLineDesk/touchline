Touchline is fiction. It is an in-universe sports desk and record office for a Football Manager 2022 save in which sixteen USL Academy college clubs play a single division. Crests are Touchline’s own drawings, not any real institution’s marks.

Every table, result, appearance, goal, rating and ability figure is read from the save on the date shown on each page. Grades, odds, board ranks and the Timeline are this desk’s own work and are labelled as such. No real player is quoted.
